Help center

Quick answers to common questions about Analytical.

Getting started

How do I create an account?

Tap Create account, enter your full name, a username, your email, your country and a password, then agree to the Terms. We will email you a confirmation link. Open it, then log in.

Is it free to join?

Yes. Creating an account and building a profile is free. If paid features such as selling projects open in future, separate terms will be shown to you before you use them.

I did not get my confirmation email

Check your spam or junk folder first. Then try to log in. If your email is not confirmed yet, a button appears that lets you resend the email. You can resend it once every minute.

Your account

I forgot my password

Go to Forgot password, enter your email and we will send a reset link. Open the link right away, because it expires after a while and can only be used once.

My reset link says it will not work

The link has expired or was already used. Request a new one from the Forgot password page, and open only the newest email you receive.

Can I change my username?

No. Usernames cannot be changed, because they form the link to your public profile. You can change your name, headline, bio, photo, country, skills and links at any time on the Edit profile page.

How do I delete my account?

Send an account deletion request from the Contact page, writing from the email address on your account. We may need to confirm that it is really you before we delete anything.

Your profile

Who can see my profile?

Everyone. Your profile is public, including to people who are not logged in, and it can appear in search. This includes your username, name, headline, bio, skills, photo, country, links and join date. Your email address and password are never shown.

What photo can I upload?

A JPG, PNG or WebP image up to 2 MB. Upload it from the Edit profile page and tap Save changes.

How do I get found in search?

Search looks at names, usernames, headlines and skills. To be easier to find:

  • Add a clear headline that says what you do
  • List your main skills, such as SQL or Python
  • Select your country

The profile strength bar on your dashboard shows what is still missing.

The platform

Why are some sections marked "Coming soon"?

Analytical opens in phases, and a section only goes live once it is fully built and working. You can see what is open on the About page.

How do I report a problem or an abusive profile?

Use the Contact page and choose Report a problem. Include the link to the page so we can find it quickly.

How is my information used?

Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for the full details.
