Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Who we are

Analytical is a platform for coders, data analysts, data scientists and other tech people. It is operated by Nexabay Technologies, based in Nigeria. In this policy, "we", "us" and "Analytical" mean Nexabay Technologies in its role as operator of the Analytical platform.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who can see it and what choices you have. By creating an account or using Analytical, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us when you sign up:

  • Your full name, username and email address
  • Your country
  • Your password, which is stored in a scrambled (hashed) form that we cannot read

Information you choose to add to your profile:

  • A headline, a bio and a list of skills
  • A profile photo
  • Links to your website, GitHub or LinkedIn

Information created by the platform:

  • The date your account was created
  • Your reputation score, verified status and role on the platform
  • Technical information needed to keep you logged in and keep the service secure, such as login sessions and request details like your IP address

3. What other people can see

Your profile is public. Anyone, including people who are not logged in, can see your username, full name, headline, bio, skills, profile photo, country, links, reputation, verified status, role and the date you joined. Your profile can also appear in Analytical search results.

Your email address and password are never shown on your public profile. Only add information to your profile that you are comfortable sharing publicly.

4. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and log you in
  • To show your profile and let you appear in search
  • To send emails that are needed to run your account, such as confirming your email address and resetting your password
  • To keep the platform safe, prevent abuse and enforce our Terms of Service
  • To understand problems and improve the platform

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers to run Analytical. They only process information on our behalf to provide their service:

  • Supabase, which provides our database, account login and file storage
  • Vercel, which hosts the website
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which deliver fonts and code libraries to your browser. When your browser loads these, the provider can see your IP address

We may also share information when the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our members and the platform.

When paid features such as the marketplace and payouts open, payments will be handled by third-party payment providers, and we will update this policy before then.

6. Cookies and local storage

Analytical stores a small amount of information in your browser so the site works properly: your login session, and your light or dark theme choice. We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers. If we add them in the future, we will update this policy first.

7. Where your information is processed

Our service providers may store and process information on servers in countries outside your own, including outside Nigeria. By using Analytical, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those countries.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal information, except where we need to keep some of it to meet legal obligations or to resolve disputes and prevent abuse.

9. Your rights and choices

You can:

  • See and update most of your information at any time on the Edit profile page
  • Ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your account and personal information
  • Object to, or ask us to limit, certain uses of your information

To make a request, use the details on our Contact page. We may need to confirm who you are first. If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or the data protection authority in your own country.

10. Security

We protect your information with measures such as encrypted connections, hashed passwords and access rules that stop members from changing each other's data. No online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please choose a strong, unique password and keep it private.

11. Age requirement

Analytical is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, please contact us and we will remove it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make important changes, we will tell members on the platform or by email. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

13. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, visit our Contact page.
