About Analytical

A global home for the people who build with code and data.

Why we are building this

Talent in tech is everywhere, but opportunity, learning and income are not shared out evenly. Good developers, analysts and data scientists are often hard to find, and hard to hire, because their work is scattered across many places.

Analytical is our answer: one place where tech people can show their work, sell what they build, find jobs, learn from each other and solve problems together, from any country.

Who it is for

  • Developers, designers and other creators who want their work to be seen
  • Data analysts and data scientists who want to share and sell their skills
  • Tutors and mentors who want to teach
  • Employers and recruiters looking for real, proven talent
  • Learners who want to ask questions and grow

What is open today

You can create a free account, build a public profile and portfolio page with your skills and links, upload a profile photo, and be found through search. Everything else is opening in phases.

What is coming

Each part of the platform opens only when it is fully built and working.

How we build

We would rather open a feature late and working than early and empty. Every part of Analytical is built to do real work: real accounts, real data and real security. Features that are not ready are clearly marked "Coming soon".

Who runs it

Analytical is a product of Nexabay Technologies, a technology company based in Nigeria, building digital platforms for people in Africa and around the world.
